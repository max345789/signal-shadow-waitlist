# Baton

> **Platform Role**: This product is a listing and introduction platform. It does not verify listings beyond the named badges, take part in deals, hold funds, provide contracts, or give legal, tax, or financial advice. Buyers and sellers do their own due diligence.

**Mission**: Make buying or selling a business clear and well organised. Every screen answers two questions: *What has been checked, and what hasn't?* and *What happens next?*

## Core Loops

- **Buyer**: describe thesis, see matched businesses, check badges and seller-reported figures, request details, talk in the Connect room, close outside the platform.
- **Seller**: guided valuation, connect accounts, auto-built listing, qualified buyers, Connect room.

## Design System

Strict Section 4 solid color tokens:
- Deep Navy `#0B2545`
- Trust Blue `#1E56A0`
- Cloud `#F5F7FA`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Ink `#1F2933`
- Slate `#52606D`
- Mist `#CBD2D9`
- Verified Green `#1B7F5C`
- Caution Amber `#B7791F`
- Brick Red `#B42318`
- Pale Blue `#E8EEF6`

Zero gradients, zero drop-shadows, zero neon, zero pastel fills. Maximum 3 non-neutral colors per view.
Responsive at 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1280, and 1536px without horizontal scrolling.
